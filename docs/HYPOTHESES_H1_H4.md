# H1-H4 assessment and required next experiment

The user supplied these hypotheses after the completed sample had been collected and its results reviewed. This dated reporting amendment changes the interpretation and presentation, not the frozen prompts, decisions, sample or accounting. It is not a retrospective claim of preregistration.

**H1:** Within an information-sharing environment, a binding decision-time constraint increases the underweighting of outstanding replenishment orders relative to adequate decision time.

**H2:** Within an information-sharing environment, supply chains operating under binding decision-time constraints incur higher total inventory-holding and backlog costs than supply chains operating with adequate decision time.

**H3:** Within an information-sharing environment and under a binding decision-time constraint, participants receiving causal education about the origins and propagation of order fluctuations underweight outstanding replenishment orders less than participants receiving standard game instructions and a matched control lecture.

**H4:** Within an information-sharing environment and under a binding decision-time constraint, supply chains receiving causal education about the origins and propagation of order fluctuations incur lower total inventory-holding and backlog costs than supply chains receiving standard game instructions and a matched control lecture.

## Available evidence

The manual sample shares inventory information and contrasts fictional 10/180-second deadline wording. H1 has a pipeline-sensitivity proxy, not an identified structural underweighting coefficient. H2 has reconstructed inventory/backlog costs. No education/control lecture groups were allocated, and decision deadlines were not enforced or timed. H3 and H4 therefore have no observed contrast. The earlier bullwhip outcome must not be relabelled as either education hypothesis.

The API studies instead manipulate access to additional reconsideration. Their measured generation differences are not verified human decision deadlines. They also lack the required education/control groups. Keep the API and manual evidence separate.

## Minimal new design

| Group | Decision time | Instruction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Adequate | Standard rules plus matched control lecture |
| B | Verified binding | Standard rules plus matched control lecture |
| C | Verified binding | Standard rules plus causal education |

B versus A addresses H1/H2. C versus B addresses H3/H4. A fourth adequate-time/education group is optional for a full factorial interaction question, rather than necessary for the four stated contrasts.

Keep information sharing the same across groups and specify exactly what is visible. Randomize chains, match the lectures' length and presentation, freeze demand paths/sample/analysis before outcomes, and validate constraint binding and education comprehension. Do not tell participants the desired bias or order. Prespecify the underweighting measure and its interpretation; a two-state sensitivity proxy is not automatically a structural coefficient. Disclose automatic continuation in cost scoring. LLM computation restrictions support model-specific claims; claims about students need human validation.

This is a design recommendation, not an authorized new run. The completed 20-response sample is retained. [Current report](../reports/CURRENT_BEER_GAME/REPORT.md).
