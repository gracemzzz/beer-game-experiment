# Development pilot figures

1. **Outcomes.** All three pilots have pipeline sensitivity 1.000. Cost, order variation and bullwhip are descriptive averages of the received decisions. C has reported source exposure and cannot establish an education effect.

2. **Cumulative cost.** Mean holding plus backlog cost through 24 weeks for the same prepared demand block. The three chats are one matched development block; no p-values or confidence intervals are estimated.

Model: GPT-6 Astra / ultra, user-reported. A has two decision opportunities and a control reading; B has one opportunity and the control; C has one opportunity and causal education. Opportunity count is not a verified binding deadline.
